Request Account or Data Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of an account or personal data associated with an application or game published by Fennec Tech.

Select the relevant application, provide the email address associated with your account, and choose the type of request.

Provide an address that helps us identify your account. Do not send your password or verification codes.
Important: This form prepares an email using your device's email application. It does not submit a request to a server. Your request is not sent until you send that email. Deletion is not automatic and will require processing.

How the Process Works

  1. Select the application and type of deletion request.
  2. Prepare and send the email using your email application.
  3. Fennec Tech will review the request and may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.
  4. Once the request has been verified, the applicable account and eligible associated data will be deleted according to the application's data handling procedures.
  5. You will be notified when processing is completed, subject to the contact information provided.

If your selected application uses a separate account system, your request will need to be processed in that system. Submitting a request through this page does not itself delete an account.

What Data May Be Deleted?

Depending on the selected application and the data it actually stores, deletion may include:

  • Account and authentication records.
  • Profile information associated with the account.
  • Personal information and user-generated content.
  • Application activity, progress, or related records.
  • Other eligible personal data linked to the account.

The exact data categories depend on the application. Data that must be retained for a valid legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reason may be retained for the applicable period, where permitted or required by law.

Data Retention

Where certain data cannot be deleted immediately or must be retained, the applicable reason and retention period will be explained when required by law or upon request.
