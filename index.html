Delete Your Fennec Athlete Account

You can request deletion of your Fennec Athlete account and all associated personal data. To initiate a deletion request, fill out the form below and click the button. This will open your email client with a pre-filled request.

Clicking this button will open your default email client. No data is sent to a server.

Alternative Contact Method

Data Deletion Information

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information associated with your Fennec Athlete account may be removed:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile information
  • Training-related personal information
  • Attendance information associated with your account
  • Other personal data associated with your account

We will confirm with you once your account and associated data have been deleted.

Data Retention

If certain information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or regulatory reasons, the applicable retention period and reason will be disclosed to you upon request or as required by law.
